About Me

A life spent in words
and in the wild.

I am a Hindi language educator from Nargund, Karnataka, with over a decade of experience teaching at high schools, government institutions, and degree colleges. Holding a B.Ed and an MA in Hindi, I have guided students across multiple boards and institutions — from the Navodaya Vidyalaya system to private colleges in the Gadag district.

But beyond the classroom, I am a hobbyist photographer with a deep love for the natural world. My camera is my second language — one I use to speak about Karnataka's forests, its skies, its insects, and its quiet country roads.

"Every frame is a lesson in stillness and attention."

I am based in Nargund, Gadag District — a town surrounded by the northern Karnataka landscape that has endlessly inspired both my teaching philosophy and my photography.
